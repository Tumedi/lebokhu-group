# LeKhuBo Connect — Advert Script, Storyboard & Promo Page

Two things here:
1. **`promo.html`** — a ready-made animated advert you can **screen-record** into a video.
2. **The script/storyboard below** — for voiceover, captions, or shooting your own.

---

## Part A — Use the animated promo page (fastest video)

`promo.html` is a self-running ~24-second animated advert in your brand colours
(navy + gold + teal) and logo, sized **9:16 (portrait)** for Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp status.

### How to turn it into a video
1. Open **https://lekhubo-connect.co.za/promo.html** (or the local file) in a browser.
2. Record the animation as it plays (it loops every 24s; click **↻ Replay** to restart):
   - **Phone:** use the built-in **screen recorder**, point-and-record the page fullscreen.
   - **Windows:** Xbox Game Bar (`Win+G`) or **OBS Studio** (free) → Window Capture.
   - **Mac:** `Shift+Cmd+5` → record the browser window/region.
3. Trim to one clean loop, add music, and export. Free editors: **CapCut, Canva, iMovie**.
4. Post it. Recommended length for a first ad: **15–30 seconds**.

> Tip: For a 15s cut, record and keep scenes 1→2→5→6. For the full story, use all 6.

## Part B — Script / storyboard (voiceover + on-screen text)

**Total run time: ~24s (six 4-second scenes). Music: upbeat, hopeful, afrobeat/house.**

### Scene 1 — Brand open (0:00–0:04)
- **Visual:** LeKhuBo Connect logo pops in on the navy gradient; "Proudly South African".
- **On-screen text:** LeKhuBo Connect
- **Voiceover:** "LeKhuBo Connect."

### Scene 2 — The hook (0:04–0:08)
- **Visual:** 🔎 icon; headline.
- **On-screen text:** "Looking for work? Need a reliable hand?"
- **Voiceover:** "Looking for work? Or need a reliable hand near you?"

### Scene 3 — Job seekers (0:08–0:12)
- **Visual:** 👤 icon.
- **On-screen text:** "For Job Seekers — find local & domestic work."
- **Voiceover:** "Job seekers — find local and domestic work. Qualified or not, everyone deserves a chance."

### Scene 4 — Service providers (0:12–0:16)
- **Visual:** 🛠️ icon + service pills (Salon, Gardening, Barber, Painting, Cleaning, Transport).
- **On-screen text:** "For Service Providers — list free & get hired."
- **Voiceover:** "Skilled workers — list your service for free, and get found by people who need you."

### Scene 5 — Hire with confidence (0:16–0:20)
- **Visual:** 🤝 icon; "⭐ Reviewed & Trusted" pill.
- **On-screen text:** "Hire with confidence. Search, chat, connect."
- **Voiceover:** "Need to hire? Search trusted local providers, chat directly, and connect — all in one app."

### Scene 6 — Call to action (0:20–0:24)
- **Visual:** logo; gold "Get Started — It's Free" button; the URL.
- **On-screen text:** "Join LeKhuBo Connect today · lekhubo-connect.co.za"
- **Voiceover:** "Join LeKhuBo Connect today. It's free. Visit lekhubo-connect dot co dot za."

## Part D — AI text-to-video (alternative, no screen recording)
Paste the **Part B script** into a tool like **Canva (Magic → text to video)**,
**InVideo AI**, or **CapCut**. Prompt them with:
> "A 30-second upbeat advert for 'LeKhuBo Connect', a South African app connecting
> job seekers with local work and people with trusted local services (cleaning,
> gardening, barbering, painting, transport). Navy, gold and teal colours. End with
> 'Join free at lekhubo-connect.co.za'." Then paste the scene voiceover lines.
