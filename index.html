Who We Are

Connecting local talent with the people who need it

LeKhuBo Connect is a proudly South African, 100% black youth-owned platform. We do two things exceptionally well: we connect job seekers with local and domestic work, and we help homeowners find trusted service providers in their area.

Whether you are highly qualified or just starting out with no formal qualifications, we believe you deserve a chance. Our role is to open the door — and to walk alongside you as you step through it.

  • Local & domestic job opportunities for job seekers
  • Service providers list their skills and get hired
  • Homeowners search and connect with trusted local help
  • Youth-focused, community-driven, expanding over time

Our Mission

To fight youth unemployment in South Africa by matching every willing person with honest opportunity — and every company with the resources they need to succeed.

Our Story

Built by youth, for youth

LeKhuBo Connect was founded by a young South African who studied B.Tech in Information Technology at the Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) and now works as a Solution Architect at one of South Africa's Big 5 banks.

Having navigated the journey from student to professional, our founder understands exactly how hard it is to break into the job market — especially for young people without connections or experience. LeKhuBo Connect is the answer to that struggle: a company that opens doors, so that no one is left behind.

“Our motto is to see you all doing something about your lives — and to see companies hiring our youth to beat the unemployment rate in South Africa.”
