Who We Are

A resourcing bridge between talent and business

LeBoKhu Group is a proudly South African, 100% black youth-owned resourcing and recruitment company. We do two things exceptionally well: we source and outsource resources that companies need to grow, and we connect job seekers with opportunities that change their lives.

Whether you are highly qualified or just starting out with no formal qualifications, we believe you deserve a chance. Our role is to open the door — and to walk alongside you as you step through it.

  • Talent sourcing for skilled & unskilled roles
  • Outsourced resources & staffing for companies
  • Youth-focused, community-driven placement
  • Multi-industry reach, expanding over time

Our Story

Built by youth, for youth

LeBoKhu Group was founded by a young South African who studied B.Tech in Information Technology at the Tshwane University of Technology (TUT) and now works as a Solution Architect at one of South Africa's Big 5 banks.

Having navigated the journey from student to professional, our founder understands exactly how hard it is to break into the job market — especially for young people without connections or experience. LeBoKhu Group is the answer to that struggle: a company that opens doors, so that no one is left behind.

“Our motto is to see you all doing something about your lives — and to see companies hiring our youth to beat the unemployment rate in South Africa.”
